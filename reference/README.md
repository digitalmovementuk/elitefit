# EliteFit operational reference library

These are historical working documents from the earlier local project workspace. They help the team find research, design decisions, copy, technical specifications and integration notes. Older dates, scope statements and instructions may have changed.

The current project plan takes precedence: website Months 1–4, full technical integration Months 5–7, and SEO Months 5–12 after website approval. The first 20 SEO pages are delivered across Months 5–7; later production volume is still to be agreed.

Imported on 27 October 2026: **416 documents** and **1358 supporting files**. The supporting files keep the documents readable. Displayed images retain their original bytes. Raw video and large unreferenced evidence archives remain local.

Agency sales material, commercial agreements, agency prices and credentials are outside this operations library. Links to unavailable historical attachments are shown as plain text. Historical drafts do not authorise publication or real payments.
