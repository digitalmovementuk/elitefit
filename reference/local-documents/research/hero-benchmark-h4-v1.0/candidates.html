Hero benchmark H4: award scouts, candidate list

Version: v1.0 — 2026-10-01

Scope: 2025 and 2026 award-level homepages with a premium, distinctive hero that is relevant to, or translatable to, a Bali gym with pool, sauna, café and a concrete-and-timber interior. Discovery used Firecrawl (10 credits: 2 searches, 6 page scrapes of award listings; Serper is out of credits). Each URL was then loaded in Playwright to confirm it is live and is the brand's own site; award pages were read in Playwright for date and award tier. Written before the full capture; the first four sites had already started when it was written.

Tiers: SOTD = Awwwards Site of the Day, HM = Awwwards Honorable Mention (lowest Awwwards tier, kept only where the sector fit is strong), FWA of the Day = thefwa.com. "Nominee" entries on Awwwards were excluded as not yet awarded.

Capture outcome

All 16 candidates were captured on mobile (WebKit) and desktop (Chromium); none was blocked. Weak points, recorded in each hero.json: La Revoltosa shows a flat red or white screen for 10 to 47 s (unstable between runs), Sobha mobile screenshots timed out (scroll states are frame grabs), and four sites kept a consent banner that offers no reject option (Performance Lab, Son Daven, Radian, Sobha).

Firecrawl accounting

Shared-account balance before this job: 141 credits. Own usage: 10 credits (2 searches at 2 credits each, 6 page scrapes at 1 credit each, one of which hit a 404 page on cssdesignawards.com). Balance at the end of the job: 94 credits (the account is shared with other agents, so the difference is larger than this job's 10 credits). No Firecrawl call was made after discovery; the machine was overloaded for part of the job and the remaining work used Playwright only.
