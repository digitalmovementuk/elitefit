Mobile customer-journey benchmark A — Elite Fit Bali

Version: v1.0 — 2026-10-01

7 gym / studio sites on an iPhone-class WebKit (390x844 @2x, real taps). J0 landing, J1 today's schedule, J2 day pass / trial, J3 membership, J4 shop. Evidence only: counts, measurements, screenshots. One folder per site beside this file (screenshots + journey.json).

Method and caveats

  • Device: Playwright 1.63 WebKit, iPhone 14 profile with 390x844 viewport, deviceScaleFactor 2, isMobile, hasTouch; en-US for US sites, en-GB + Europe/London for UK sites. Machine IP geolocates to Bali, Indonesia (some shops and checkouts localised to IDR / en-id).
  • Taps are real touch taps (locator.tap or touchscreen.tap). A scroll is one swipe-equivalent of about 650 px (window scroll in 600 ms steps); a fast flick could need fewer. Consent and popup taps are counted separately and are not in the journey tap counts. Typed text was a city name only.
  • Seconds are rough: measured browser time per tap on that site (navigation + 1.2 s settle) + 1.2 s per scroll + 2 s per typed entry. They exclude human aim time.
  • Hard rules kept: no account created, no personal data entered, no payment card, no form submitted, no purchase, no newsletter. Each funnel was stopped at the first screen asking for personal or payment data. Adding a merch item to a cart drawer and opening the Shopify checkout page was done; nothing was typed there.
  • Transferred bytes: resource timing (same-origin only, cross-origin shows 0) and Playwright response sizes (all hosts, best effort). Wallet buttons were checked in the rendered checkout (wallet iframes); Apple Pay does not render in this desktop-host WebKit, so its absence is not proof.
  • Screenshots: <site>/NN-step.png (2x pixels); files named detour/failed/cloudflare are evidence of a tap miss or a challenge page. Visual review of screenshots was partial (image reads were throttled); layout facts come from rendered-DOM measurements (rects, fixed/sticky elements, hit tests).

Cross-site synthesis

  1. 1. Fast schedule = one persistent Book CTA plus a schedule that renders right under the location: SoulCycle needs 2 taps and 0 scrolls (studio page lists the next ~10 classes with Reserve); Barry's 4 taps (hero search > studio > Schedule tab) with spots-left badges; 1Rebel and Third Space open a default scope (all clubs / preselected club) after 3 taps.
  2. 2. Location is the main schedule cost: Equinox needs 5 taps + 11 scrolls (117 clubs, 43 in New York). Defaults (NYC preselected, ALL CLUBS, Clapham Junction) remove it.
  3. 3. Pass purchase starts fast when the first-timer offer sits on one screen with price and validity: SoulCycle $25 / $50 / $60 is 1 tap from the hero, 1Rebel £59 is 1 tap from the banner, Barry's $75 3-pack is the first pricing card, Gymbox free trial shows £25 > £0.
  4. 4. Price at the point of choice works (Gymbox from-price on every club card, 1Rebel per-session price); price hidden in an FAQ (Third Space) or only after step 2 (Equinox) does not.
  5. 5. Account walls come before any payment screen at SoulCycle, 1Rebel and Barry's (1Rebel create-account = 15 fields); only Gymbox lets a guest reach the details step (trial form about 9 fields, join step 4).
  6. 6. Wallet buttons (Shop Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) appeared only in the Shopify merch checkouts, never in the class / membership funnels before the account step; Apple Pay could not be shown by this desktop-host WebKit.
  7. 7. Every merch shop is a separate Shopify domain with its own consent prompt (Barry's also a newsletter popup), 16-24 px icons and a cart drawer; the class funnels and the shops run on different domains and carts.
  8. 8. Anti-patterns measured: country splash with no content (1Rebel); consent sheets over 40-64% of the first viewport (Equinox, Barry's, 1Rebel, Gymbox); timed enquiry popup after consent (Third Space); targets under 44 px (Third Space MENU 15 px, SoulCycle shop quantity 7x7, Barry's header pill 26 px, shop icons 16-22 px).

  11. 11. Best to copy for (a) SCHEDULE ACCESS: SoulCycle (2 taps, classes on the studio page), Barry's (spots-left + day focus strip), 1Rebel (default all clubs + time-of-day chips).
