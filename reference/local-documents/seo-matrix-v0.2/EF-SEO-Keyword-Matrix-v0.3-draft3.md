### 1. Give Elite Fit its own “Gym in Canggu” page: the homepage is carrying these searches alone

*Strike while close · Memberships · effort Medium: one new page*

Elite Fit already shows up for these searches, but only on pages 2–3 of Google and only through a homepage with almost no text and three unrelated headings. A page that says what Elite Fit offers in Canggu and Pererenan gives Google a clear answer, and none of the five competitors has built one.

- **Data:** 3,560 searches/mo (Bali, 5 terms) · KD 30–47 · competitor pages for 0 of 5 terms (none of the five) · Elite Fit today: #20 (gym pererenan) · best competitor: Reload Sanctuary #1 for “gym canggu”
- New page with the heading “Gym in Canggu & Pererenan” on the address /gym-canggu/.
- Cover what a member gets: equipment, classes, recovery area, café, opening hours, prices from the membership page, map and how to get there. Real photos, not stock.
- Link it from the main menu, the homepage and every page about training; link back to the membership page.
- End with a short question-and-answer block (parking, guest passes, what to bring) so it can also appear in Google’s question boxes.

Terms: #2 gym canggu, #6 gym bali canggu, #7 gym pererenan, #8 fitness canggu, #5 best gym canggu

### 2. Publish a real day-pass page and take the day-pass searches from Body Factory

*Beat a thin page · Day passes · effort Medium: one new page*

Elite Fit already sells day passes, but only inside a promotion page. The one competitor with a day-pass page, Body Factory, has a page with almost no text, plus one short page per pass length (1, 3, 7 and 14 days). Body Factory leads Google for the day-pass searches that were read live. A page that answers what a visitor needs to decide (price, what is included, how to get in) can take that place, and every sale is direct revenue.

- **Data:** 500 searches/mo (Bali, 9 terms, 4 n.a.) · KD 21–26 · competitor pages for 5 of 9 terms (Body Factory (pass / membership, 25 words, thin)) · Elite Fit today: #12 (gym week pass) · best competitor: Body Factory #1 for “gym day pass”
- New page with the heading “Gym Day Pass in Canggu, Bali” on the address /gym-day-pass/.
- Show every pass in one table: single day, duo offer, week and longer stays, with what each includes.
- Add short answers to the three questions visitors have: can I just turn up, do I need to book, what should I bring.
- Add a section for week-long visitors and digital nomads on the same page instead of separate pages.
- Link to it from the homepage, the menu, and from the recovery and café pages, so every visitor who looks at sauna or brunch also sees a pass.

Terms: #13 gym day pass, #14 gyms with day passes, #15 day pass gym near me, #18 gym day pass canggu, #19 gym day pass bali, #16 gym 1 day pass, #17 gym week pass, #20 weekly gym pass bali, #21 digital nomad gym canggu

- **Data:** 7,300 searches/mo (Bali, 6 terms) · KD 16–34 · competitor pages for 3 of 6 terms (Nirvana Life (service page, 1,750 words), Body Factory (blog guide, 1,051 words), Obsidian (blog guide, 522 words)) · Elite Fit today: #3 (calisthenics near me) · best competitor: Body Factory #1 for “ice bath near me”

- Add fresh photos of the sauna, the ice bath, the classes and the café every month.

Terms: #22 sauna near me, #23 ice bath near me, #15 day pass gym near me, #31 calisthenics near me, #34 personal trainer near me, #26 gym with sauna near me

### 10. Hold the “gym Bali” spot and add a best-gym guide: the biggest demand in the table

*Longer play · Memberships, Day passes · effort Larger: a guide page and links from the others*

“Gym Bali” is the biggest single number in the table and no competitor has a page built for it.
