02 — Recommended scope

What this phase includes

Test GymMaster’s standard website module first for memberships, signups, forms, timetable and class/PT bookings. It may cover the core gym journey without a custom checkout build.

Treat ESB as a separate order route until Patrick confirms what it is used for and ESB confirms product fit. Do not assume the earlier Xendit/custom-connection concept is required.

Included in the integration add-on: product discovery; GymMaster module setup for the agreed journey; ESB capability check; one approved order/payment route if supported; confirmed record handoff; acceptance checks and handover.

Separate scope unless priced and approved: custom ESB development; forcing unsupported on-page payment; merchandise/catalogue migration; automatic renewals; historical contact import; two-way attendance/booking sync; CRM/WhatsApp/SMS automation; provider fees; ongoing maintenance.

05 — ESB vendor questions

Questions to close before a fixed scope

Action: The client-side contact reaches out to ESB; Mohammed reviews the reply and updates feasibility, scope and effort before build approval.

  1. What exact ESB product and edition does Elite Fit use: Order QS, Order FS or another product?
  2. Can it represent gym memberships, day passes and class/PT products, or only its documented restaurant order types?
  3. Can Elite Fit’s website create and price an order through the API? What test account or credentials are needed?
  4. Can payment stay within the website? If not, where does the customer go?
  5. What does ESB return after a paid, failed or cancelled payment? Is there a status check or automatic notice?
  6. Can a completed order create a GymMaster member/membership? Is there an existing connection or extra fee?
  7. Which payment methods, refunds, fees, account requirements and support commitments apply to Elite Fit?
