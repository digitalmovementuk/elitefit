Version: v2.0 — 2026-10-01 · owner: integrator (Step 3) · every known deviation from Raoul's brief, _build/CONTRACT.md (v1.1), _build/SECTIONS.md and the research, one line each with the reason. Open point for Raoul (one question)

Duo day pass: does IDR 225.000 per person apply to all three passes (Perform, Elite, Recharge)? The page shows it for all three because the promo page shows a single Duo day price (BRAND-NOTES item 5, "Duo day pass 225.000 each"). Per tier it is unconfirmed: a Duo Recharge day would be 75.000 below its single day pass, a Duo Perform day 125.000 below. passes.json carries it as duo.dayPassTiers (marked as an assumption, API-CONTRACT question 10).

B10The first-view transfer is measured from navigation to load + 2 s; the hero clip, which starts after load and idle (about 20–40 ms after load on P1), is reported on its own line and in the total.CONTRACT §8 definition. On P1: first view 372.7 KB, clip 1,274.9 KB, total 1,647.7 KB.
B11Lane JS is 34.1 KB gzip as shipped (comments and indentation stripped by build.mjs), above the 30 KB guidance of stack-shop §8.No minifier or library may be added (CONTRACT §1); 42.4 KB gzip as authored. This is guidance, not a CONTRACT §8 budget.
B12The pages here are served by python3 -m http.server, which does not compress; every byte figure is the uncompressed transfer.Local preview; a real host with gzip or brotli sends about a third of the text bytes.

Integration step (_build/integration-report.md has before/after for each)

#DeviationReason
I1The page carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">.Concept preview, not live (README). In WordPress AIOSEO owns robots; the staging copy stays noindexed, production must not.
I2The mock checkout opens only on local hosts (localhost, 127.0.0.1, file:, *.local, *.test) or hosts listed in EF_CONFIG.previewHosts; anywhere else every "Buy a pass" opens a pre-filled WhatsApp chat and #checkout is hidden. node build.mjs --wp prints checkout.php only when EF_HOME_CHECKOUT === 'concept'.WP-MAPPING §1 hard gate: the mock must not reach the public domain.
I3tokens.css fallbacks are four weight-specific faces (Syne 600 and 700 on Arial Bold, Hanken ≤ 500 and ≥ 600 on Arial) instead of one per family.Font-swap shifts: the H2s fell back to Arial Regular 21 % too narrow.
I4Hero lede max-width 20.5rem (was 21rem); hero card price one line at 26 px below 1280; "EF Hero Body Fallback" 99 % (was 99.8 %).Font-swap shifts at 390 (rewrapped lede), 1024 (card grew 27 px) and the desktop nav. Known defect in the snapshot: at exactly 759 px the 99 % face makes the lede one line shorter than Syne, so a late font swap grows the hero board by 26 px (forced-swap CLS 0.012); the fix (back to 99.8 %) is listed for v2.1 in _build/integration-report.md §7.
I5The hero <video> has no poster attribute; the poster <img> underneath is the picture until the clip shows a frame.Chromium named the <video> as the LCP element; now the preloaded <img> is named (CONTRACT §8: "never the video").
