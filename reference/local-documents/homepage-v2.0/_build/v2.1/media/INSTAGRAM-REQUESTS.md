# Elite Fit Bali homepage v2.1: what to ask the club for (Instagram and original files)

Version: v2.1 — 2026-10-01
Changelog: 2026-10-01 created by lane C-src. Nobody at the club was contacted; this file is for Raoul.

## 1. What Instagram exposed publicly

Nothing through the allowed route. The Firecrawl CLI scrape of https://www.instagram.com/elitefitbali/ returned "we do not support this site" and no content. No login, no account, no session, no downloader site and no workaround was used or tried.

What is public through the club's own website: the plugin feed on /perform/ and /recharge/ lists 10 reels from 15 Oct to 1 Nov 2024 (URL, caption, date, still image on the club's server). Their video links expired on 2024-11-05. The list is in SOURCING.md, section 3.2. None of the 10 is usable for a slot (skits with captions, a dog, a ghost costume, review overlays) and none names Ain, Icha, Martijn, Putu or Rey. The club's current reels are not visible to this lane.

## 2. Why a request is still worth it

The club's own media library already gave us 3 videos (SOURCING.md). `Header-Portrait.mov` covers every zone and room with a clean shot, but each shot is a single cut of 0.6 to 2.1 s, and the three spa rooms are the shortest. The page wants calm 3 to 5 s loops. Unedited originals of those shots would let the loops run in real time instead of slowed or ping-ponged. No trainer clip exists anywhere.

## 4. What to ask for, in one block

- The original exported files, not Instagram downloads: MP4 or MOV, 1080p or higher, 24 to 30 fps, vertical 4:5 or 9:16 preferred.
- No text, captions, promo signs, logo animations or music; silent files are fine.
- Each shot at least 6 s so a 3 to 5 s loop needs no slow motion.
- A short written OK that the clips (and any guests or staff in them) may be shown on the proposal website preview; a release for guests before launch.
