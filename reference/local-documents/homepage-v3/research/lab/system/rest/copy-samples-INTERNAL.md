OUR APPROACH

Using 3D motion capture and force plate technology, we analyse how your body moves, where force is lost, and what's creating injury risk — giving you a precise picture of your athletic baseline that no assessment can match.

The result is a clear picture of what's actually limiting your performance — not assumptions, but real data about how your body moves and functions.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

We don’t do one-size-fits-all testing. Come in for a single assessment, test our full range, or bring your team — in-lab or fully remote. Either way, our edge is in how we analyze the data.

BOOK A CONSULTATION

Diagnostic assessment

01

A comprehensive baseline evaluation before any programming or treatment begins. We look at your movement history, injury background, and performance goals to determine exactly what needs to be tested and why.

3D motion capture analysis

02

Full tracking of your athletic movement in three dimensions — identifying positioning, coordination patterns, and inefficiencies that are invisible to the naked eye but directly impact your performance and injury risk.

Force plate testing

03

Research-grade 3D force plates measure how your body generates and absorbs force — giving us a precise picture of your power output, stability, and neuromuscular performance that no visual assessment can replicate.

ShapeScale body composition scanning

04

3D body composition analysis providing exact measurements of body fat percentage, muscle distribution, and body metrics. Track meaningful changes in your composition over time.

Speed and reactivity testing

05

Speed, reflexes, and responsiveness assessment using precision timing gates. Measures neuromuscular health, coordination, and reaction time — important predictors of athletic performance.

Performance report

06

A full written breakdown of your results — your strengths, your weaknesses, your movement quality, and a clear set of actionable recommendations specific to your sport and your goals. No raw data dumps, just answers.

Follow-up consultation

07

A dedicated session with your specialist to walk through every finding in your report, answer your questions, and map out the most effective path forward based on what the data shows.
