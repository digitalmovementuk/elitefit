Type scale in both faces

Hanken Grotesk (display and body, 400 and 500 only) and Fragment Mono (one weight, uppercase UI). Phone sizes are fixed; from 760 px the display sizes follow the measured curve (rem × vw/120) while body stays at 16 px or more and mono at 12 px or more. Elite Fit extensions no reference equivalent

The reference has no timetable, no temperature readout, no cafe, no price tiers and no nav sheet with data. These are built in its language (mono labels, hairlines, one accent, rectangles) and are marked as extensions in the system document.
