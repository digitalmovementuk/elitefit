2. Name rules

  • Raoul's pillar names, used as given: Elite Nutrition (the cafe), Elite Training (the Perform side), Elite Rest (the Recharge side). The club's pages do not use these names (searched 2026-10-02); the club says "Perform" and "Recharge" for the two sides, "indoor cafe", "outdoor restaurant" and "Coffee & Protein bar" for food. Invent no further product names.
  • Tiers: Perform, Elite, Recharge (the club's names). Club labels: Elite = "BEST DEAL" (membership page) and "All Access · Most Popular" (Better Together page). Duo = the club's word for two people (F-PRC-05).
  • Club words allowed as names: Elite Fit Bali; Better Together (the club's campaign name); GymMaster app (class booking); ClassPass (non-members); Day pass; Class Pass.
  • The word "Elite" appears only in the tier and the three pillar names, never as a boast. Club wording that is a guard word is marked [guard] in the rows.
  • People: Rey, Ain, Icha, Tinos by first name only (F-COA-01 to F-COA-07).
