Interaction timing at 4x CPU comes from the baseline harness (_tools/measure.mjs, _build/actions-v3.json), see MOTION-V3.md "Measured". Raw results: research/motion/parity/*.json.

Overflow (defect reported by the hero lane, fixed 14:40, narrowed 15:28): the footer wordmark's second line and its pan-blur echo arrive from the right and pushed the document to 448 px at 390. The footer now clips horizontally while the engine runs (overflow-x: clip); titles and echoes in the sections arrive from the left and stay inside the gutter, so the sections need no clip (a clip on every section was tried first and cost frames around the sticky parts).
