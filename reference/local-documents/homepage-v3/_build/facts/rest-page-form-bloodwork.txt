## FAQS
How long until I see results?
Most clients report noticeable improvements in energy and focus within 2-3 weeks, with optimal results at 3 months. Your personalized 12-week protocol will include milestone tracking to measure your progress.
Is the testing process complicated?
Not at all. Our nurses come to your home or office at your preferred time, and the entire blood draw process takes less than 15 minutes. We handle all logistics and lab coordination.
How does the insurance coverage work?
We provide all necessary documentation for insurance claims. Most international insurance providers cover our services. Our team can guide you through the reimbursement process, just hit the Chat button to speak with us.
What exactly do you test for?
Our comprehensive panels analyze over 50 biomarkers including testosterone (free and total), cortisol, thyroid function, vitamin D, inflammation markers, and metabolic health indicators. Each package is tailored to your specific goals.
How often should I get tested?
For optimal results, we recommend quarterly testing to track progress and adjust your protocol. However, this varies based on your goals and initial results. Our specialists will recommend the ideal frequency for your situation.
Is this just for athletes or bodybuilders?
No, our clients include executives, entrepreneurs, professionals, and anyone looking to optimize their performance and energy levels. Our protocols are customized to your lifestyle and goals.
What if I'm already taking supplements?
Our testing will help verify if your current supplements are working and identify any gaps or adjustments needed. Many clients find they can optimize their supplement routine and often reduce unnecessary products.
How private is my health data?
We maintain strict medical-grade privacy standards. Your data is encrypted and secured according to international healthcare privacy regulations. Only you and your assigned healthcare providers can access your information.
What support do I get after testing?
You’ll receive a detailed report with actionable insights, a one-on-one consultation to review your results, a personalized optimization protocol, and ongoing support through our private community. Premium packages include regular check-ins with our specialists.
Do I need to prepare for the testing?
Most tests require 8-12 hours of fasting beforehand. We’ll provide detailed instructions based on your selected package and schedule your blood draw at the most convenient time for your routine.
What if I travel frequently?
We can work around your schedule and coordinate testing dates that align with your time in Bali. Our digital platform allows you to access your results and consult with our team from anywhere in the world.
Do you have payment plans available?
Yes, we offer flexible payment options and can work with your insurance provider for direct billing in many cases. We also have corporate wellness packages for companies.
