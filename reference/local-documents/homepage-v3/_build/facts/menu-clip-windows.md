Version: v3.0 — 2026-10-02 · lane: menu · internal. Cut times come from ffmpeg scene detection (plus or minus 0.15 s); every window starts about 0.15 s after a cut and ends 0.15 s before the next. The nutrition lane confirms in and out by eye when it encodes. Source files are in `_inputs/footage/`.
