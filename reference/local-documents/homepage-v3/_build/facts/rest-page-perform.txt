## FAQS
What is included in the Perform membership?
Perform members can access our indoor gym, outdoor gym, all classes, water, showers & lockers, towel, and exclusive discounts.
Are group classes included in Perform membership?
Yes, group classes are included starting from our weekly Perform membership
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, you can freeze your membership (max 1 month) with a freeze charge of 200k IDR.
Do you have any discount for couples?
We do have discount for couples who join our monthly membership up to 18%
Can I transfer my membership to my friend?
Yes, members are able to transfer their membeship one time to a friend with additional charge of 200k IDR.
How do I book a class?
Members can book classes through the Gymmaster app and at our front desk (for classes options, we have them on our website and IG @elitefitbali) non-members can still join our classes via ClassPass application.
