## FAQS
What is included in the Recharge facilities?
Recharge facilities include our pool deck, jacuzzi, ice bath, sauna, turkish steam room, water, shower & lockers, towel, sports treatment rooms, and exclusive discounts.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, you can freeze your membership (Max 1 month) with a freeeze charge of 200k IDR.
Can I use the gym area with this membership?
Unfortunately, Recharge membership is only available for the recovery area. Recharge Members will also be able to use the gym area if they purchased our Elite membership.
Do I need to bring my own towel?
You don’t need to bring your own towel (though you could) since we will provide towel for our members.
What is the ice bath temperature?
The ice bath temperature is 4-6 degrees
What is the sauna and steam room temperature?
Sauna temp ranges 80 — 100
Steam room 38 — 40
Ice Bath 4 — 6
Jacuzzi 38 — 40
