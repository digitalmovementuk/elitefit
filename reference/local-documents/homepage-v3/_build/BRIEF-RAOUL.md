# Raoul's brief for homepage v3 (verbatim, 2026-10-02)

Version: v3.0 — 2026-10-02 · source of truth for every lane. Changelog: 2026-10-02 saved verbatim by the orchestrator.

Hi Michael. Build the Elite Fit Bali homepage v3 as a focused prototype: the look, feel and typographic form of theperformancelab.ca, above all its hero, with the facts, content, voice and logo of Elite Fit, optimized as a SALES ASSET for day passes and memberships. v3 is its own project in homepage-v3/ (version stamp v3.0, changelog on every document). It is shown to the client next to the current design as two design directions: Direction A = the existing v2.1 (homepage-v2.0/, untouched), Direction B = v3. Mobile first. Nothing is published or sent.

## PROTOTYPE SCOPE (Phase 1: only these sections, in this order; everything else waits for Raoul's "go")
- Hero (the Lab's hero, below) with a minimal header: EF logo, Buy a pass, menu icon (the full menu sheet comes in Phase 2), plus the review line.
- 1) Classes: today's classes from the real timetable.
- 2) The pillars of Elite Fit, three blocks in this order, each with one real club clip and a short scannable list of facts: Elite Nutrition (the cafe: the "create your own bowl" showcase, the grab-and-go fridge, drinks, the dietitian on request), Elite Training (the gym floor and equipment brands, classes included, the coaches by name, opening hours), Elite Rest (pool deck and loungers, sauna, steam, jacuzzi, ice bath with their temperatures, treatments on request, opening hours). The pillar names are Raoul's: use them as given, invent no further product names, take every fact from the club's own pages (verify against the live site; the three pillars map to the club's cafe, its Perform side and its Recharge side).
- 3) Pricing: Perform, Elite and Recharge by length, in the sales pattern below.
- Also in Phase 1: the always-visible WhatsApp button and the sticky buy bar on phones. Not in Phase 1: the menu sheet, the pinned temperature box, coaches, review section, visit, footer, full checkout concept, the expanded motion layer, the full QA.

## WHO IT IS FOR, AND HOW IT SOUNDS (Raoul is a member and knows the club first hand; his brief is the first source)
- The club: everything feels personal. It is a space where people can be themselves. It has everything to perform and reach goals, but it is not centred on performance and not about showing off. It is for expats and long-stay residents who want a home in Bali.
- So the page speaks like a welcoming place, not like a performance brand: personal and direct (talks to one person), calm and unforced confidence, community and belonging ("a place to call home" is the club's own line in its reel caption and "Better Together" film), practical for people who live here (flexible passes from a day to 52 weeks, partner discounts around Canggu, opening hours, WhatsApp with real people, real coaches by name), goals supported without theatre.
- Voice guard: no hype, no status, no competition words ("elite athlete", "beast", "crush", "shred", "no excuses", "exclusive", "luxury", "VIP", transformation promises); the word Elite appears only in the product and pillar names and is never used as a boast. No clinical, testing or performance-outcome claims, no new superlatives or promises.
- VOICE-GUIDE-ELITEFIT.md (+ html): Raoul's brief verbatim at the top, then evidence: the club's own pages, reel captions, and the verified Google reviews' wording (member language), then do and don't rules and ten example lines. From The Performance Lab only the FORM carries over: brevity, rhythm, the way labels, buttons and numbers are written and typeset; never its register or wording.

## HERO: THE LAB'S HERO, ADOPTED (Raoul especially likes it; it is the signature of v3)
- Study it first and in motion (priority one of the Lab measurement; per research/hero-benchmark-spec-v1.0.md: a filmstrip every 0.5 s for 8 s on load plus three scroll states, WebKit 390x844 and Chromium 1440x900): what the oversized headline does (crop at the viewport edge, scale, weight, tracking, how it moves), the photography treatment (motion blur, crop, colour grade, overlay), the monospace UI (labels and readouts, and what they say), what moves on load and on scroll, how the header behaves over the hero, the phone composition.
- Re-create the same composition and behaviour with Elite Fit's content: an oversized headline cropped by the viewport edge, in Elite Fit's own words (short, chosen from FACTS and the voice guide; the full text stays in the h1 for screen readers and the crop hides only a small part of the letters, so the words stay readable at a glance), over full-bleed real club footage given the same kind of motion-blur treatment (made from the club's own clips or photos, directional blur or long-exposure feel; never the Lab's photo), with the monospace UI as live readouts in Elite Fit's terms: Pererenan local time (WITA), open until 22:00, next class with countdown, classes today. The readouts are real data from the timetable and the hours.
- The sales job stays inside that hero: Buy a pass, Today's classes and the review line (Google 4.7, 488 reviews, one verified 5-star quote at a time, verbatim from reviews.json; unverified quotes are not shown) are visible in the first phone view; hero about two thirds of the first viewport (the cropped headline fits that height: crop harder, do not make it taller); LCP element = the hero poster; the clip starts after load; nothing blocks input. Phone hero text centred unless it breaks the direction (flag it).
- Hard but warm: the hero keeps the Lab's energy, while the footage shows rooms and people being themselves, not performance posturing.
- Opus 5.5 builds the hero and its motion. Evidence for the Fable verdict: the Elite Fit hero and the Lab hero filmstrips side by side. Provenance: none of the Lab's imagery, no font file.

## PROJECT RULES
- v3 builds on no other project: no partial, stylesheet, script, build file or motion engine of v2.x is copied or imported. Own structure, own build (a plain folder served directly is fine), own motion layer. v2.x documents (MOTION-AND-DESIGN.md, MOTION-UX-AUDIT.md) may be read as a behaviour spec only.
- Allowed inputs, because they are the club's own: its public pages, media library, class timetable, menu, prices, hours, Google rating and reviews. Data and media already collected (homepage-v2.0/assets/data/schedule.club.json, passes.json, reviews.json; the club's photos; the master footage in homepage-v2.0/_build/v2.1/media/sourcing/originals/) may be copied into homepage-v3/ as raw inputs, each logged with its origin in INPUTS.md. Re-read the live club pages for anything that may have changed.
- Nothing is published, pushed or sent. No account, no contact with the club or with the reference site.

## BRAND SPLIT (the rule for every decision)
- Look, feel and the form of the copy = The Performance Lab: palette, type system (our benchmark noted oversized, edge-cropped headlines and a monospace UI), layout, components, imagery treatment, spacing, header patterns, motion level, brevity and rhythm of the text. Measured from the live site, never guessed.
- Facts, content, voice and identity = Elite Fit: every fact, the voice above, the EF logo as the club uses it (taupe mark; a mono variant only where contrast demands it, flagged), the club's real photos and clips.
- Stays out (belongs to The Performance Lab): its name, logo, photos, video, icon files, code, licensed font files, and its sentences: no sentence, headline or label is copied. No requests to its domains. Nothing implies affiliation.
- Font: open-licence family = use exactly, self-hosted, recorded in assets/fonts/LICENSES.md; commercial family = closest open match for the prototype plus the licence route (foundry, type, indicative price) for Raoul. Buying anything needs Raoul's explicit approval.
- Not adopted, because Raoul wants native scroll responsiveness and our house rules forbid it: Lenis-style smooth-scroll hijacking and any modal, cookie or pop-up overlay within the first 3 seconds.
- Where the Lab's hard, experimental look could fight the club's warmth (oversized cropped type, monospace data styling, dark surfaces), keep the look and soften the content: real faces, real rooms, human words. Record each such call in DECISIONS.md.

## COPY PROCESS
- FACTS.md (+ html): the fact sheet for the Phase 1 sections only, every fact with its source URL or data file: opening hours, address, class list and times, equipment brands, coaches, facilities and temperatures, treatments, menu items and prices, pass and membership prices, offers, rating and reviews. The only content the writer may use.
- The page copy is written by a fresh Fable 5.1 agent that sees only VOICE-GUIDE-ELITEFIT, FACTS and the section list (the house copy process). All checks run afterwards as a separate pass.
- COPY-LEDGER.md (+ html): every visible string, the fact IDs it rests on, nothing added, nothing lost. Strings without a source are removed. A script compares every number (price, time, temperature, hour) with the data files. English, plain, for the visitor; no internal wording on the page.

## THE SECTIONS IN DETAIL
- Classes: the real timetable (36 classes a week, one hour each), day tabs with a sliding indicator, live now/next with countdown, no invented seat numbers, class rows that arrive with a crisp stagger on a day change.
- Elite Nutrition: source the club's real food offer first: its menu online (find it on elitefitbali.com and the club's other public pages; Serper is empty, so Firecrawl CLI; expected credits at most 6 of the shared balance, announce before use, check the balance before and after; no Instagram login, no workaround). Exact names, descriptions and prices; never invent an item or a price; gaps are listed. Showcase "create your own bowl" as a tappable step flow (base, protein, toppings, sauce, as the real menu defines them) with the running price when the menu gives prices; the action is "Ask or order at the cafe" or a WhatsApp message with the chosen bowl in the text. A showcase, not an order form: no payment, no account.
- Elite Training and Elite Rest: facts and clips as listed under the scope; Elite Rest shows the four rooms with their temperatures (ice bath 4-6, jacuzzi 38-40, steam 38-40, sauna 80-100) as a compact readout row in the Lab's data style (the pinned phone version comes in Phase 2).
- Clips: the club's real footage as short silent loops (master footage in homepage-v2.0/_build/v2.1/media/sourcing/originals/; cafe scenes around 6-14 s and 42 s of the reel), lazy, poster first, at most 2 playing on a phone; stills where no footage exists.
- Pricing: Perform, Elite, Recharge by length (day, week, 4, 12, 26, 52 weeks) with the Duo option, real price on every card, what is included, all three tiers in ONE phone viewport, the length selector pinned under the header while the section is on screen and released when it ends, price morphing on change, one primary button per card that opens a WhatsApp message with the chosen tier and length (the full checkout concept comes in Phase 2). Show the real offers the club publishes (elitefitbali.com/offers-discounts/; only live, published offers) and the partner-discount note, and a short trust stack (4.7 on Google with 488 reviews, hours, location, what is included).
- WhatsApp button: always visible bottom-right on phones, lifting above the buy bar, with a clearly noticeable pulse; number to be confirmed by Raoul before launch. Sticky buy bar on phones: day-pass price and Buy a pass.
- First view transfer 900 KB or less, CLS 0.

## MOTION (Opus 5.5 at maximum effort owns it across the page and in the hero)
- v2.1's motion, effects and scroll responsiveness were good and are the benchmark: v3 re-creates the v2.1 behaviours that apply to the Phase 1 sections in its own engine, never weaker: hero arrival, header condensing, anchor jump with destination highlight, class-day switch with sliding indicator and staggered rows, now/next countdown, price morph and odometer, selected-card travel, sticky selector, buy bar slide, tile reveals and light parallax on clips, counters, review rotation, WhatsApp pulse, press states, focus rings. Frame rate and responsiveness are measured against v2.1 at the same throttle and never lower.
- Expand where it has a UX job (orient, guide, confirm, explain, build desire, make waiting feel shorter), in the Lab's language: the hero's oversized type and blur treatment carried through the section headlines (crop, slide and scale with the scroll), scroll-driven section transitions, count-ups and data-style readouts, image and clip masks that open as they enter, ingredient chips landing in the bowl with the running price morphing. Native scrolling, no scroll-jacking. Welcoming: precise and quick, never aggressive.
- Rules: transform and opacity (anything else only if measured at 60 fps), interruptible, UI feedback 600 ms or less, atmospheric reveals 900 ms or less, never delays a primary action, reduced motion and data-saver keep layout and information, at most 3 simultaneous animations and 2 clips on a phone, JS kept small (budget set at step 1 and reported). MOTION-V3.md (every moment: UX job, trigger, timing, phone version, reduced-motion version), written alongside the build.
