## Not changed, with the reason

- The hero, the header and the menu sheet are their lanes' motion; this audit only checks them.
- Menu sheet timings in the harness (`menu-sheet-close`, `menu-anchor-jump-far` respond in about 315-325 ms) are the menu lane's design: the sheet closes first (220 ms exit, state `closed` is written after it), then the page jumps. Flagged to the orchestrator in MOTION-V3.md, not a motion-layer cost.

Overflow (defect reported by the hero lane, fixed 14:40, narrowed 15:28): the footer wordmark's second line and its pan-blur echo arrive from the right and pushed the document to 448 px at 390. The footer now clips horizontally while the engine runs (`overflow-x: clip`); titles and echoes in the sections arrive from the left and stay inside the gutter, so the sections need no clip (a clip on every section was tried first and cost frames around the sticky parts). Document and body width equal the viewport at 320, 360, 375, 390, 414, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, at load and through a full scroll, in WebKit and Chromium, with and without the motion layer (own check and the harness overflow section).
